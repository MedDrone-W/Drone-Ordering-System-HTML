Arasaka Biomedical

Arasaka Biomedical is a website for ordering prescription medicines to be delivered by drone. Users sign up and log in, upload their prescriptions, and get texts when their delivery is on its way and when it arrives. A map shows the drone’s estimated arrival time using Google Maps and live drone data, and an AI assistant helps users with questions.
